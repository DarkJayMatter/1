What MFIN Does

As an Industry Association and SRO, MFIN’s primary objective is to work towards the robust development of the microfinance sector.

  • Inclusive Growth

    Advancing an inclusive microfinance ecosystem through advocacy, collaboration and industry development, while expanding opportunities for underserved communities across India.

  • Financial Empowerment

    Equipping borrowers with the financial awareness, knowledge and tools they need, while promoting responsible lending and client protection.

  • Stronger Tomorrow

    Strengthening the microfinance industry through self-regulation, capacity building, market intelligence and responsible practices.
