Questions people ask

How many funded startups are there in Pune?

1,601 of Pune's 19,787 registered companies (8.1%) carry a funding signal of some kind in our 2026-08 snapshot. "Some kind" is doing real work in that sentence: 1 are named on an investor portfolio, 45 are named in an incubator portfolio, 2 are reported to a state startup scheme, 6 are named in an accelerator batch, 1,568 are self-declared on the government form, unverified. We hold no licensed funding feed, so this is an evidence trail and not a round history.

What does "on a state scheme" mean on this map?

15 companies in Pune are registered with a state startup scheme. That is a government-recorded fact and it is not evidence that anyone funded them: a scheme registration says a state department has them on file, nothing more. They appear on this map because they are evidenced, placed, publishable companies, and they are counted separately from the 1,601 carrying a funding signal.

Is every logo on this map one company?

Every logo is one company, and a logo with a "+" count stands for the companies too close to it to draw apart at this zoom; zooming in separates them. 605 are drawn, the ones placed at a locality or a street. 699 more are known only to the district, so they open as a list from the map instead: a logo at the district centre would read as an address. The panel says which resolution it is showing you.

Does a funding signal mean the company is still trading?

No. Registration is not an operating signal. A large share of registered startups are dormant, and we have no way to tell you which. A funding signal is evidence that money was raised at some point, or that the company said so on a government form. It is not a statement about today. How we counted.
